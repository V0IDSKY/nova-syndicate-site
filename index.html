07 / FIELD NOTES

Digital hygiene

Principles

Security is a habit

  • Limit unnecessary metadata and minimize sensitive data retention.
  • Prefer strong defaults: MFA, encrypted transport, strict validation, and least privilege.
  • Document decisions with evidence, not assumptions.
  • Review system boundaries, access models, and logging before deployment.
Practice

Minimize disclosure

  • Use synthetic data in demonstrations and workshops.
  • Review access scopes and retention before sharing artifacts.
  • Favor strong session controls and auditability.
  • Respect privacy and consent in every research workflow.
