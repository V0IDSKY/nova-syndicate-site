05 / About NØVA

Independently founded. Quietly sharp.

“NØVA SYNDICATE is an independently founded digital collective focused on cybersecurity education, digital awareness, technology and creative research.”

Founded by VØIDSKY.

NØVA currently has one founder. There are no employees, members, certifications, or formal partnerships to claim. The archive is intentionally minimal and transparent: it teaches the principles, tools, and tradecraft behind security without pretending to be a large institution.

Knowledge. Independence. Curiosity.

Any fictional narratives and simulated incidents are clearly labeled as fiction throughout the archive.

06 / Privacy & Security

Visitor-first, by design.

We do not collect unnecessary personal information, and we do not expose visitor data publicly. This platform is built to be static and lightweight, with no hidden tracking, credential collection, or unnecessary analytics. No passwords or sensitive credentials are requested or stored.

  • No hidden tracking or behavioral profiling.
  • No public exposure of visitor information.
  • No credential theft or unauthorized access tooling.
  • Any future user-submitted content must be handled securely and minimally.
